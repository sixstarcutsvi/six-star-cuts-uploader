Six Star Cuts Uploader

A private desktop tool that uploads and schedules videos to one YouTube channel, @sixstarcutsvi. Only the channel owner uses it. There is no public sign-up.

What it does with YouTube

It uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's edited videos, set their publish times, confirm the right channel is connected, and read the status and view counts of videos it uploaded.

An AI agent (Claude, by Anthropic) helps choose, edit and title clips and runs the tool under the owner's standing instruction.

See the Privacy Policy, the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
