Privacy Policy

Effective: November 25, 2026. Contact: sixstarcutsvi@gmail.com.

Six Star Cuts Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data accessed

  • Upload permission (youtube.upload), to upload and schedule videos.
  • Read permission (youtube.readonly), to confirm the channel handle and read the status and view counts of videos uploaded with the tool.

Data stored

On the owner's computer: the Google access token, video IDs, publish times, video status and view counts. Passwords are never seen or stored. The tool sets no cookies and collects nothing from website visitors. The website's host may keep its own logs.

How data is used and shared

Data is used only to run the channel. The AI agent may process channel status and view counts to operate the tool and report results, so that data is sent to its provider, Anthropic. Data is not sold or used for advertising. Uploads go to YouTube.

Retention and deletion

Status and view counts are refreshed or deleted within 30 days. The tool's disconnect command asks Google to revoke access, then deletes the token and the stored YouTube data from its files. Reports kept by the AI service are deleted separately by the owner under that provider's settings. Access can also be revoked at any time in Google security settings. Deletion questions: sixstarcutsvi@gmail.com.
